Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Oneway Taxi Service and how does it work?

With our Oneway Taxi Service, you only pay for the distance traveled from your pickup location in Bangalore to your destination city (e.g., Bangalore to Mysore or Ooty). You do NOT have to pay any return charges or double fares!
