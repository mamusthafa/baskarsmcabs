Frequently Asked Questions

What areas are covered in Coorg Local Sightseeing?

Our standard local sightseeing covers Madikeri Fort, Raja's Seat, Abbey Falls, Dubare Elephant Camp, Bylakuppe Golden Temple (Kushalnagar), and Nisargadhama. We can also customize trips to Talakaveri or Bhagamandala.

How can I book a taxi with SM Cabs Coorg?

Booking is extremely straightforward! Fill out our booking query form to submit details directly over WhatsApp, call our helpline at +91 6361659021, or send us a text. We will quickly align on pricing and driver allocation.

Which is the nearest airport to Coorg and do you provide transfers?

The nearest airports are Mysore Airport (MYQ - ~120 km), Mangalore Airport (IXE - ~140 km), and Bangalore Airport (BLR - ~280 km). We provide dedicated transfers to and from all these airports with advance booking.

Are toll charges and parking fees included in the fare?

We believe in transparent billing. Our quotes outline per-km charges and driver allowance. State toll taxes, parking fees, and entry fees are paid as actuals during the journey, or can be included upon request.

Are your drivers experienced on mountain roads?

Absolutely! All SM Cabs Coorg drivers are highly experienced in navigating ghat roads, hairpin bends, and misty conditions typical of Coorg and neighboring regions like Ooty and Wayanad.
